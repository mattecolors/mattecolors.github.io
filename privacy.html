Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant app used only by its developer on their own computer, with their own Google accounts.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): message senders, subjects and text, to decide what is important and to find dates and deadlines.
  • Google Calendar: reading calendars to answer schedule questions, and creating events only on a separate calendar named "Jarvis".
  • Google Tasks: creating and reading to-do items with due dates.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own Windows PC and stored locally in an encrypted or user-protected local database. There is no Jarvis server.
  • Short excerpts of messages may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to summarize them or extract dates, under Anthropic's terms. They are not used for advertising.
  • Google sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows data protection and never leave the PC.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for anything other than the features above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Jarvis folder on the PC removes all locally stored data.
