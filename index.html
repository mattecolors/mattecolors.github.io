Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal desktop assistant for Windows, built by one person for their own use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks so it can summarize important mail, add deadlines to a dedicated "Jarvis" calendar, and answer questions about the owner's schedule. It never sends email or deletes anything on its own.
